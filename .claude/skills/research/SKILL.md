# Research Skill

Deep research using web search and site crawling, pre-loaded with StockSight AI business context.

## How to Use

```
/research <topic or question>
```

**Examples:**
- `/research best social media channels for fintech pre-launch in 2025`
- `/research how retail traders discover new financial tools`
- `/research competitor analysis for real-time market intelligence platforms`
- `/research fintech app launch strategy for B2C`

---

## Instructions (Follow Every Time)

### Step 1 — Understand the query

Parse what Adam is asking. Is this:
- **Competitive intelligence** (who else is doing this, how are they positioned)?
- **Marketing/growth research** (channels, strategies, what's working)?
- **Audience research** (retail trader behavior, pain points, watering holes)?
- **Technical research** (stack, tools, platforms)?
- **Regulatory/compliance** (UK/US fintech rules, financial promotions)?
- **Industry/trend research** (market signals, macro context)?

### Step 2 — Run deep, multi-pass research

Do not do a single search and summarise it. Go deep:

1. **Broad search first** — get the landscape
2. **Targeted follow-up searches** — dig into specific angles that matter
3. **Crawl key sources** — use Firecrawl to pull the full content of the most relevant pages, not just snippets
4. **Cross-reference** — look for contradictions, consensus, and outliers

For each topic, run at least 3-5 distinct searches. For complex topics, more. Examples for "competitor analysis for market intelligence platforms":
- Search 1: Who are the main competitors for real-time market intelligence platforms for retail traders?
- Search 2: Unusual Whales, Benzinga Pro, Bloomberg Terminal pricing and positioning
- Search 3: What retail traders say about existing market intelligence tools (Reddit, forums, reviews)
- Search 4: Fastest growing fintech tools for retail traders 2024 2025
- Crawl: Top competitor landing pages for positioning language and feature claims

### Step 3 — Apply StockSight AI business context

Always filter findings through this lens:

**Who Adam is:** Solo founder, pre-launch, based in the UK (GMT+1/BST), building StockSight AI
**What StockSight AI is:** Real-time market intelligence platform that detects market-moving signals and explains their impact before the broader market reacts
**Target audience:** Retail traders and investors who feel they're always late to market moves
**Current stage:** No revenue, no users — building website and app, capturing sign-up interest
**Resources:** Solo founder — budget and time are constrained
**Positioning:** Signal before the move. Not noise. Not "get rich quick." Professional, intelligent, minimal.

When interpreting research, always ask:
- What does this mean specifically for a pre-launch B2C fintech?
- What's realistic for a solo founder with limited budget?
- Does this apply to retail traders in particular?
- What's the UK angle if relevant?
- Is there a competitive opportunity here for StockSight AI?

### Step 4 — Synthesise and present

Use this output format:

---

*[Date]*

**TL;DR**
2-3 sentences. The single most important takeaway and what Adam should do with it.

**Key Findings**
- Finding 1 — with brief explanation of why it matters
- Finding 2
- Finding 3
*(As many as needed — quality over quantity)*

**What This Means for StockSight AI**
- Specific implication for the business, product, or marketing
- Another implication
*(Frame findings through Adam's specific situation)*

**Recommended Actions**
- [ ] Concrete action item
- [ ] Another action item
*(Only include things Adam can actually act on given his stage and resources)*

**Sources**
List key URLs and sources used

---

## Notes

- Prioritise recent sources (2024-2025) unless historical context is explicitly needed
- If research surfaces a major risk or opportunity Adam should know about urgently, lead with it
- Run as many searches as the topic warrants — don't cut short for efficiency
- For regulatory topics (UK/US financial promotions, FCA rules, investment advice disclaimers), always flag specific risks clearly
- When the topic is ready for Perplexity (paid), note it — some deep research passes will benefit from it in future
