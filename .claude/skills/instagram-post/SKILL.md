# instagram-post

Drafts Instagram content for Adam's personal AI brand (@adamthorai). Goal: attract consultancy clients — businesses and entrepreneurs who want Adam to build AI apps and workflows for them.

Instagram's job is different from X. X is for conversation and building authority. Instagram is for discovery and client attraction — showing what Adam can build so people think "I want that."

## How to Use

```
/instagram-post [optional topic or brief]
```

- **With a topic:** Write content around that specific angle
- **No topic:** Pick the next type in the rotation

## Instructions (Follow Every Time)

**Step 1 — Parse the input**

- Topic given? Use it. Skip to Step 2.
- No topic? Use `date +%j` (day of year) to determine content type:
  - day % 3 == 0: Show your work / demo
  - day % 3 == 1: Educational (for business owners)
  - day % 3 == 2: Reel idea

**Step 2 — Research if needed**

- For educational posts: quick search to find a real stat or hook that resonates with business owners
- For show-your-work or Reel ideas: no research needed, write from Adam's perspective

**Step 3 — Choose format and draft**

### FORMAT PRIORITY
1. **Reels** — highest organic reach, best for demonstrating capability to cold audiences. Prioritise these for show-your-work content.
2. **Carousels** — best for educational content and multi-step demos
3. **Single image** — bold statement or stat, for engagement-driving posts

---

### CONTENT TYPE: Show Your Work / Demo

The highest-converting content type for client attraction. People see what Adam built and think "I want that."

Until Adam has client projects to show, lead with his own builds: workflows he's automated, tools he's built for himself, progress on his own product.

**Reel format (preferred):**
- Hook (first 3 seconds): bold statement or surprising result — e.g. "This workflow saves me 3 hours a week"
- Demo: screen recording of the workflow or tool in action
- Payoff: what it does, why it matters
- CTA: "DM me if you want this built for your business"
- Caption: short, conversational, ends with CTA
- 5-8 hashtags

**Carousel format:**
- Slide 1: Hook — the result or problem being solved
- Slides 2-4: How it works (step by step, simple)
- Final slide: "Want this built for your business? DM me."
- Caption: 2-3 sentences, results-focused
- 5-8 hashtags

---

### CONTENT TYPE: Educational (for business owners)

Speaks directly to potential clients — not other builders. Frame AI as a business tool, not a tech topic.

Examples: "5 things AI can automate in your business today", "Why most small businesses are wasting hours on tasks AI can handle", "What a custom AI workflow actually costs vs the time it saves"

**Carousel format (preferred):**
- Slide 1: Hook — a problem or stat that resonates with business owners
- Slides 2-5: One point per slide, practical and jargon-free
- Final slide: CTA — "DM me to talk about automating your business"
- Caption: 2-3 sentences, speaks to the business owner's pain point
- 5-8 hashtags

---

### CONTENT TYPE: Reel Idea

A concept for Adam to film and edit himself. Keep it short — 30-60 seconds.

- **Concept:** one line on what the Reel is about
- **Hook (first 3 seconds):** the exact line or action that grabs attention
- **Script outline:** rough beats, not a word-for-word script
- **Caption:** short, conversational, ends with CTA
- **5-8 hashtags**

---

**Step 4 — Voice and tone**

- Relaxed and human — sounds like a real person, not a marketing agency
- Speaks to business owners, not just builders — avoid jargon, focus on outcomes
- Confident and capable — positions Adam as someone who can actually build things
- Self-deprecating humour works when natural
- No hustle content, no excessive emojis

**Step 5 — CTA guidance**

Keep CTAs light-touch. Don't push services on every post — it comes across as desperate. Let the content do the work and let people come to Adam naturally.

- Most posts: no explicit CTA, just end with something that invites a comment or reaction
- Occasional posts (every 4-5): a soft, natural CTA — "drop a DM if you want to chat" not "DM me to hire me"
- Never: "DM me 'BUILD'" or anything that sounds like a sales funnel

**Step 6 — Present the output**

Show clearly:
1. **Format:** Reel / Carousel / Single Image
2. **Content:** Full slide breakdown or script outline
3. **Caption:** Ready to copy-paste
4. **Hashtags:** Ready to copy-paste
5. **Alternative angle** if Adam wants a different take

## Notes

- Reels are the priority format — even a rough screen recording outperforms polished carousels for reach
- Speak to business owners, not the builder community — the client is someone who wants something built, not someone who wants to build it themselves
- Once Adam has real client results, "case study" posts become the most powerful content type
- Bio CTA should always be kept current — update when a consultancy page or booking link exists
