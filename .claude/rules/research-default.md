# Research Default Behaviour

When Adam asks you to research, look into, investigate, find out about, or dig into any topic — in any phrasing — default to the research skill defined in `.claude/skills/research/SKILL.md`.

This applies to natural language like:
- "Research X"
- "Can you look into X"
- "What's the deal with X"
- "Find out about X"
- "Dig into X"
- "What do we know about X"
- "I want to understand X better"

Do not do a quick one-search summary. Always follow the full research skill process: multi-pass searches, crawl key sources, apply StockSight AI context, present structured findings.
