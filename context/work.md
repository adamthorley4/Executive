# StockSight AI — Business Overview

## What It Is

StockSight AI (a.k.a. StockShare AI, StockSight) is a real-time market intelligence platform that detects market-moving signals and explains their potential impact before the broader market fully reacts.

**One-liner:** StockSight AI detects market-moving signals in real time and explains their potential impact before the broader market fully reacts.

## How It Works

- Monitors global news, social media (X/Twitter), economic data releases, and geopolitical developments
- Detects "signals" — events likely to influence supply, demand, or market sentiment
- Classifies the event type (geopolitical tension, supply disruption, macro data release, corporate news, etc.)
- Maps the event to affected assets (commodities, equities, indices, sectors)
- Assigns directional bias (bullish/bearish) and a confidence level
- Generates a concise, actionable real-time alert

**Example alert:** "Iran threatens to close the Strait of Hormuz. Potential oil supply disruption — bullish for oil prices."
