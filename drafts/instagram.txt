CONCEPT
Your business is leaking time on tasks you've accepted as "just how it is" — this reel names one of them and shows what life looks like when AI handles it instead.

---

HOOK (first 3 seconds)
To camera, casual:
"Your business is doing something right now that takes hours. AI does it in 30 seconds."

---

SCRIPT OUTLINE

[0–3s] Hook — straight to camera, no intro fluff
"Your business is doing something right now that takes hours. AI does it in 30 seconds."

[3–10s] Name the problem — keep it concrete, pick one most viewers will recognise
"Someone sends you an enquiry — email, DM, contact form, whatever. Then someone reads it, figures out what they want, adds it to a spreadsheet, drafts a reply, sends it. If you're lucky it happens same day."

[10–20s] Show the contrast — cut or quick screen share
"With an AI workflow: message comes in, AI reads it, categorises it, logs it, and sends a personalised reply — all before you've even opened your inbox. Every time. No one touches it."

[20–30s] Land the point — relatable, not preachy
"Most businesses I talk to have three or four of these. They just don't know it yet because they've been doing it manually for so long it feels normal."

[30–40s] Close to camera — relaxed, direct
"I build these workflows. If you want to stop doing the stuff you shouldn't be doing, DM me — let's have a look at what we can knock out."

---

CAPTION
You've got tasks in your business that feel unavoidable. Most of them aren't.

AI can handle the inbox triaging, the lead logging, the follow-up emails — the stuff that eats your afternoon without ever moving the needle.

DM me if you want to talk about what that could look like for yours.
