CONCEPT
How much time is your team spending answering the same customer questions — over and over, every day?

---

HOOK (first 3 seconds)
Adam to camera, casual, direct:
"How much time does your team spend answering the same questions every single day?"

---

SCRIPT OUTLINE

Beat 1 — Hook (0–5s)
On camera, Adam asks the hook question. Let it sit for a beat. Slight shrug or head tilt — make it feel like a genuine question, not a pitch.

Beat 2 — The problem (5–20s)
"Most businesses I talk to have the same 15 or 20 customer questions coming in constantly. What are your hours? How does delivery work? What's the return policy? And every single one gets answered manually. By a person. Every time."
Cut to: phone notification sounds, inbox filling up — you can screen record a flooded inbox or just mime checking your phone constantly.

Beat 3 — The build (20–35s)
"I built a workflow that handles this automatically."
Cut to: quick screen recording — AI reads an incoming message, identifies the question, drafts a response, flags anything unusual for a human to check. Keep it moving, don't dwell.

Beat 4 — The result (35–50s)
Back on camera.
"The routine stuff gets handled. Your team only touches the things that actually need a human. We're talking hours back every week — for something I built in about a day."

Beat 5 — CTA (50–60s)
"If your inbox looks like a battlefield, DM me. I'll tell you if this is something I can build for you."

---

CAPTION
Your team probably spends a chunk of every day answering questions they've answered a hundred times before. There's a better way to handle that — and it doesn't require hiring anyone new.
