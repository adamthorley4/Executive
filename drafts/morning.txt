1/ Nobody explains context windows properly, so here goes:

Every time you send a message, the AI re-reads your *entire* conversation from scratch. There's no memory — just a window of text it can see at once. That's it.

2/ The window has a size limit (tokens ≈ words). When it fills up, old messages drop off the bottom. The AI literally forgets what you said at the start.
