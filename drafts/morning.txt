1/ Your AI doesn't remember anything.

Every time you hit send, the whole conversation is re-read from scratch. What feels like "memory" is just history being sent with every message.

Understanding this changes how you build.

2/ Once it clicks, you stop asking "why did it forget?" and start asking "what am I actually sending in context — and what's it costing me?"

Prompt caching exists precisely because of this. You're paying to re-read the same system prompt thousands of times a day.
