1/ Most people assume bigger context window = smarter AI. Not quite.

Models don't treat all parts of the context equally. Stuff buried in the middle gets 'lost' — less likely to influence the output than what's at the start or end.

2/ It's called the 'lost in the middle' problem and it's well-documented.

If you're building anything with long docs or heavy context — worth thinking about where you put the important bits.
