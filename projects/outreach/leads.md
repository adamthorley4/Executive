## Where to find leads

- **LinkedIn:** search "business coach Dubai", "life coach Dubai", "sales consultant Dubai" — filter to people, not companies
- **Instagram:** coaches post heavily, contact email usually in bio or website
- **Their website:** most coaches list a contact email directly

## Status key

- E1 / E2 / E3 = email sent on that day
- Reply = Y/N
